Using {{ pwa.name }}

You need an account, made with an email address you control. Keep access to that email and to any device with your passkey: either can sign in as you.

Groups

Joining a group shares your name and your days with everyone in it. Only invite people you're happy to share them with. Leave a group whenever you like (a group's owner deletes it instead). The owner of a group can remove people from it.

Connected apps

If you connect an assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT, it acts as you: it can read your own days and how many of your friends will be in each resort, and change your own days. Only connect one you trust, and disconnect it from your account page at any time.

Your content

What you say stays yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to you and your groups, which is what the service does.

Acceptable use

Don't use the service to break the law, to harm others, or to disrupt it for other people.

Availability

We try to keep the service running but can't promise it never stops. Copies kept on your device for offline use are not a backup.

Closing your account

You can delete your account at any time from your account page. That also deletes the groups you started, for everyone in them. We may close an account that breaks these terms.
