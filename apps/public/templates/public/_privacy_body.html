Privacy notice

Last updated: 13 October 2026

What we keep

  • Your email address, to identify your account and send you sign-in links and codes.
  • Your name, as you typed it, which the people in your groups see.
  • Your groups: which you belong to and which you started.
  • Your days: which resort you said you'll be in on which day. Everyone in your groups sees them.
  • Passkeys: a public key for each one you add, its name, and when it was last used. Your device keeps the private key; we never see it, or your face or fingerprint.
  • Connected apps: if you connect an assistant such as Claude, which one, and the access you gave it. You can disconnect it at any time.

On your device

The app stores pages you've opened, so they open without a connection. Signing out removes them.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in, and one protects forms from forgery. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Visitor counts

We count visits with fivebar, which sets no cookies. For each page you open it receives the page, the page you came from, your browser, device type and country, and a few events such as creating an account. It keeps daily totals only, which can't be traced back to you; your IP address and browser details tell visitors apart for one day and are not kept. Addresses that hold an invite, a sign-in link or a group are counted without it. See fivebar's privacy policy.

{% endif %}

Emails

We email you the sign-in links and codes you ask for, and, unless you turn it off, a note the evening before a day you have plans, saying where your friends will be. Every evening email has a link to stop them.

Who else sees it

The people in your groups see your name and your days. Our hosting and email providers{% if analytics.domain %}, and fivebar for visitor counts,{% endif %} process data on our behalf. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

Your rights

You can delete your account from your account page, which deletes your days and the groups you started. You can also ask us for a copy of your data.
