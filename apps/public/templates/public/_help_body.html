Help

Signing in

There are no passwords. Enter your email address on the sign-in page and we'll email you a link and a six-digit code. Open the link, or type the code on the page that asked for it; either works once, for 30 minutes. Using {{ pwa.name }} from your iPhone's home screen? Type the code: a link opens in Safari, which signs in Safari rather than the app. The first time you sign in, this creates your account and asks for the name your friends will see.

Passkeys

Once you're signed in, open your account and add a passkey. From then on you can sign in with Face ID, a fingerprint or your device PIN. You can remove a passkey from the same page.

Groups

Start a group from Groups and send its invite link to your group chat. Anyone with the link can join, and everyone in the group sees each other's names and days. The owner can reset the link, which stops the old one working, and can remove people. Anyone else can leave a group whenever they like; the owner deletes it instead.

Saying where you'll be

On the Calendar, tap a day (or select several) and pick the resort you'll be in. A day you haven't said is simply undecided. If a resort is missing, tell us and we'll add it.

Seeing who's where

The Map shows every resort, with how many friends will be there on the day you pick; tap one to see who. On the Calendar, tap one of your groups to add, in pale blue, where its people will be on the days you haven't said. Each group's Who's where shows everyone in it, a week at a time.

The evening email

The evening before a day you've said, we can email you where your friends will be. Turn it off on your account page, or from the link in any of those emails.

Asking Claude

Add {{ pwa.name }} to Claude as a custom connector, using the address on your account page, and sign in when asked. Claude can then save or clear your own days, and tell you how many friends will be in each resort on each day. It never learns who they are: {{ pwa.name }} gives it counts, not names. Disconnect it from your account page at any time.

Installing the app

On iPhone or iPad, open {{ pwa.name }} in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android or a desktop browser, use Install in the browser's menu.

Using it offline

Pages you've opened while signed in still open without a connection, as they were when you last saw them. A day sheet needs a connection to open, but once it's open you can save or clear without one: the change waits on your phone and is sent when you're back online.

Signing out

Signing out clears this device's copies of your pages, so the next person to use it can't read them. A change still waiting to send stays on the device, and is sent only if you sign back in.

Deleting your account

Delete your account from your account page. It deletes your days and the groups you started, and takes you out of everyone else's.
