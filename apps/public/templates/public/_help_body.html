Help

Signing in

There are no passwords. Enter your email address on the sign-in page and we'll send you a link. It works once, for 30 minutes. The first time you sign in, this creates your account and asks for the name your friends will see.

Passkeys

Once you're signed in, open your account and add a passkey. From then on you can sign in with Face ID, a fingerprint or your device PIN. You can remove a passkey from the same page.

Groups

Start a group and send its invite link to your group chat. Anyone with the link can join, and everyone in the group sees each other's names and days. The owner can reset the link, which stops the old one working.

Saying where you'll be

On the calendar, tap a day (or select several) and pick the resort you'll be in. A day you haven't said is simply undecided. If a resort is missing, tell us and we'll add it.

Installing the app

On iPhone or iPad, open {{ pwa.name }} in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android or a desktop browser, use Install in the browser's menu.

Using it offline

Pages you've opened while signed in still open without a connection, as they were when you last saw them.

Signing out

Signing out clears this device's offline copies, so the next person to use it can't read them.
