For skiing with friends

Who's skiing where, and when

On a season pass your friends could be at any of a hundred resorts. Say which resort you'll be in on which days, and see where your group will be over the coming weeks, without digging back through the group chat.

How it works
  1. Start a group and send the invite link to your group chat.
  2. Everyone fills in their days: which resort they'll be in.
  3. Your calendar shows which friends will be in your resort and where the others are, so meeting up is one message in your group chat.
