For skiing with friends

Who's skiing where, and where to meet

On a season pass your friends could be at any of a hundred resorts. Say which days you're skiing and where, see where your group will be, and agree one resort to meet at, without losing it in the group chat.

How it works
  1. Start a group and send the invite link to your group chat.
  2. Everyone fills in their days: which resort they'll be in.
  3. Who's where shows the week at a glance, so meeting up is one message in your group chat.
